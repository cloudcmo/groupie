GROUPIE

add it to your home screen

iPhone & iPad

  1. Open Groupie in Safari.
  2. Tap the Share button (the square with the arrow).
  3. Scroll down and tap Add to Home Screen.
  4. Tap Add. Done — it opens like an app.

Android

  1. Open Groupie in Chrome.
  2. Tap the ⋮ menu, top right.
  3. Tap Add to Home screen (or Install app).
  4. Confirm. Done — it opens like an app.

No app shop, no download, no updates. It's just the game, one tap from your thumb, every day.
